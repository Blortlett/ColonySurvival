Everyone gets one life. Including you.

120 people wake up in the wilderness with no rules, no leaders and nothing to their name. They gather, trade, steal and fight. Slowly, gangs form. Gangs become groups, and groups become kingdoms with a king at the top. Then the kingdoms go to war over land.

You can sit back and watch order grow out of anarchy, or step in as one more person in the crowd. Chop wood, haul stone, join a kingdom and try to stay alive long enough to see it rise or burn. When you die, you're gone.

Controls

  • Tab: open inventory
  • E: harvest a resource node
  • Hold F while hovering an NPC: open the social menu
  • 1 to 6: equip hotbar item
  • Scroll wheel: zoom in and out
